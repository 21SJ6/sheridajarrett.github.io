About Me

I design digital experiences that transform ideas and emotions into meaningful interactions. My curiosity leads me to explore new techniques, helping me grow creatively in both design and front-end development. At MCTC, I've focused on building responsive websites and creating visual designs that are clear and easy to use. My work often reflects calm, nature-inspired aesthetics, but I'm also drawn to playful and unexpected ideas influenced by storytelling and internet culture. I'm interested in designing and building for businesses, events, and personal projects, with a focus on work that connects with people as I continue to grow and explore new directions.

Portfolio

A collection of selected work in web design, typography, illustration, and mockups.

Cafezinho

Responsive botanical coffee shop website.

Project Overview

Cafezinho is a responsive website concept for a botanical coffee shop. The goal was to create a calm, cozy, and nature-inspired digital experience.

Tools Used

Figma, HTML, CSS, Visual Studio Code

Behind the Scenes

I focused on earthy colors, simple navigation, and a warm visual style. I wanted the design to feel welcoming while still being easy to use on desktop and mobile.

Fruit Illustration

Digital illustration with playful organic forms.

Project Overview

This fruit illustration project explores organic shapes, color, and playful visual style. The goal was to create a bright and expressive illustration that feels fun, fresh, and visually engaging.

Tools Used

Adobe Illustrator

Behind the Scenes

I worked with outlines, simplified forms, and composition to make the fruit feel clear and recognizable while still keeping a creative, illustrated style. This project helped me practice visual balance, shape-building, and color choices.

Bloom Mockup

App icon and visual identity presentation.

Project Overview

Bloom is a mood journaling app concept designed to help users reflect on their emotions in a gentle and visual way. The project uses soft colors, floral elements, and playful character-inspired visuals to make emotional tracking feel more approachable.

Tools Used

Figma, Adobe Illustrator, image mockups

Behind the Scenes

I focused on creating a calm and expressive visual identity that connects emotions with nature. The mockup was designed to show how the app could feel welcoming, personal, and easy to understand for users.
