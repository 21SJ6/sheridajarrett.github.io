About Me

I design digital experiences that transform ideas and emotions into meaningful interactions. My curiosity leads me to explore new techniques, helping me grow creatively in both design and front-end development. At MCTC, I've focused on building responsive websites and creating visual designs that are clear and easy to use. My work often reflects calm, nature-inspired aesthetics, but I'm also drawn to playful and unexpected ideas influenced by storytelling and internet culture. I'm interested in designing and building for businesses, events, and personal projects, with a focus on work that connects with people as I continue to grow and explore new directions.
